Olympus

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

Olympus is a personal productivity application. This policy describes what information the application accesses, how that information is used, and the choices available to its user.

Information accessed

With the user's authorization, Olympus connects to the user's Google account using the read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly). Through this permission the application can view the user's email messages and profile information. The application cannot send, modify, or delete email.

How information is used

Information accessed through Google is used solely to provide the application's features to the user — organizing, displaying, and summarizing the user's own email for their personal use. It is not sold or rented to anyone. It is not shared with third parties for marketing or advertising, and it is not used to build advertising profiles.

Data protection

Access is limited to what the application's features require. The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

The user may revoke the application's access at any time through the security settings of their Google account. Revoking access stops all further retrieval of information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to lp.olympus.assistant@gmail.com.
